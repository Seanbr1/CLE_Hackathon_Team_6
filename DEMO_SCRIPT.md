# Retirement Hub: demo script

About 8 minutes: roughly 2 for the slides, 5 for the demo and 1 to close.
**SAY** is spoken. **DO** is what the driver clicks.

## Before you start

- Check the backend and UI are both running, then open http://localhost:5173 at 125% zoom.
- Click ⚙ → **Restart demo**, then **▶ Start demo**. The first slide is now on screen.

---

## Presentation (about 2 min)

### Slide 1: Our solution

> **SAY:** "We're Team 6, and this is Retirement Hub: a proactive maturity and retirement journey. It opens every maturity case twelve months early, routes it to the right broker or in-house advisor, and carries the customer from her options, through advice, to documents and payment. The routine work is automated, so our teams only handle the exceptions. Three people take part: the customer, her broker and our operations team."

**DO:** Next

### Slide 2: The opportunity

> **SAY:** "Why does this matter now? The maturity wave nearly doubles our operational demand. Maturing policies rise from about 8,900 in 2026 to over 16,000 by 2034, up 82 percent. Without automation, that means a near-proportional rise in headcount. With it, our sample shows 38.5 percent of capacity released, from 3.04 to 1.87 FTE for a comparable workload. In our prototype journey, manual touches per case drop from around nine to one. And that capacity has to be in place before the peak, not after. Let us show you how it works, following one customer, Maria Schneider, on the live platform."

**DO:** Next

---

## Live demo (about 5 min)

### 1. Case opened automatically (operations view)

> **SAY:** "Twelve months before maturity, the platform has already opened Maria's case. Nobody requested it. It's also checked who owns the relationship. Maria is broker-managed, so the lead goes to her broker, not to us."

**DO:** Next

### 2. Annual statement (Maria's portal)

> **SAY:** "Seven months out, Maria receives the annual statement she'd get anyway. It now includes her maturity options and an illustrative value. There's no separate mailing."

**DO:** Click **See my options**. This also moves the demo to the next step.

### 3. Customer requests advice

> **SAY:** "Maria can explore her options online, with clear figures. Notice she can't choose one here. That's deliberate: this is information, not advice. When she's ready, she asks her broker."

**DO:** Click **Ask my advisor for advice**.

> **SAY:** "That request has gone straight to her broker as a qualified lead. No email, no phone call."

**DO:** Next

### 4. Broker-led advice (broker workspace)

> **SAY:** "Here's the broker's view. Maria's request is waiting in his queue."

**DO:** **Pick up lead**

> **SAY:** "Everything he needs is on one screen: her pot, her preference, the options she's seen."

**DO:** **Accept lead** → **Schedule conversation**

> **SAY:** "He accepts the lead and schedules the call. After the conversation, he records the option they agreed and why."

**DO:** **Record advice**

> **SAY:** "That decision is now on the record, and it automatically triggers the formal paperwork."

**DO:** Next

### 5. Formal pack and documents (Maria's portal)

> **SAY:** "The formal pack has already gone out. Nobody on our side lifted a finger. Maria just needs to upload two documents."

**DO:** **Upload passport** → **Upload bank details**

> **SAY:** "But the platform spots a problem. Her bank account is in her married name, which doesn't match the policy. So instead of paying out automatically, it holds the payment for review."

**DO:** Next

### 6. Exception handled by CLE Operations (operations view)

> **SAY:** "This is the only point where our operations team gets involved. Maria's case is in the queue, flagged red."

**DO:** **Review exception**

> **SAY:** "The case worker sees exactly what conflicts, with the evidence already assembled. One check against her passport, one click, and the case is released."

**DO:** **Approve and release**, then Next

### 7. Activity by participant (the timeline)

> **SAY:** "Here's everything that happened on Maria's case, by who did it. The platform did the routine work. Maria and her broker did what only they can do. Our operations team made one targeted intervention, compared with around nine today. Across these cases that's about 85 percent less manual handling, and people only spend time where they're actually needed."

**DO:** Next

## Close (about 1 min)

### Closing slide: Next steps

> **SAY:** "This is a working prototype. To take it to a pilot, we'd do four things. First, deliver the full journey in German for our customers and brokers. Second, add AI agents to read documents, flag conflicts and summarise cases, while every binding decision stays rule-based and auditable. Third, connect to our core systems. Fourth, pilot it with a small group of brokers and measure the results. Thank you."

---

## Likely questions, with one-line answers

- **"Is this real data?"** "It's a working prototype on synthetic data. The journey, rules and audit trail are live; the pension values are illustrative."
- **"Where's the AI?"** "It's on the roadmap, for document reading and case summaries. We deliberately kept binding decisions rule-based, which is what the spec asks for."
- **"Why isn't it in German?"** "German localisation is our first next step. The customer names and scenarios are already built for the German market."
- **"Where does 'nine touchpoints' come from?"** "It's our stated baseline assumption for today's process, not a measured figure."
